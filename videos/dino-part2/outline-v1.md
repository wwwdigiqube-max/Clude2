# Season 1 · Part 2: outline v1 (option A + Pip's family)

**Working title:** The Asteroid Hit 40 Days Ago. You Have 7 Days of Food. (Part 2)
**Length:** 11 to 12 min, about 1,800 words, 7 chapters
**Countdown:** 7 dried fish = 7 days of food
**Starts from Part 1:**
- Day 40 after the impact
- Wound on the right forearm, now healed into a scar
- Phone dead, pen dry, the book, the bone shovel
- Small fire at the stone door, Pip

## 0 · The Wall (hook)
Day 47. The last fish is gone. You scrape something white off the tunnel wall and look at it in the firelight. Pip is watching you. "Seven days ago, you had seven fish."

## 1 · Seven Fish
- Day 40, the inventory on the wall.
- Cold, grey ash falling like snow. The firewood is running out.
- One fish a day: the countdown is set.

## 2 · The Grey World
- First time outside: a dead forest, silence, no birds.
- The river is grey and cold, and the fish are hard to find.
- The crocodile is still there. It survived too.

## 3 · Eat What Eats the Dead
- Pip digs in a rotting log: beetle larvae.
- Real science: on land and in rivers, the food chains that lived on dead things kept going.
- Rule four: eat what eats the dead.

## 4 · The Book
- The wood is wet and the fire is dying.
- One by one, you burn the pages of the book. You keep only the cover with the skeleton.

## 5 · The Mushroom Forest
- The dead forest turns white with fungus.
- Real science: in the rock layer right after the impact, scientists find a layer full of fungus spores.
- Which ones are safe? Pip refuses one kind, and you follow Pip.

## 6 · Small Mouths
- Pip's nest: five tiny pups.
- Now you share everything you find.

## 7 · Green (ending)
- Day 100. Something green pushes through the ash: a fern.
- Real science: right after the fungus layer comes a layer full of fern spores, the "fern spike".
- Bittersweet ending: Pip leaves with his family to dig his own tunnel. "Stay small… but don't stay alone."
- The wall: "Friends: one" becomes "Friends: one. And five more, somewhere out there."
- Comment question: would you have burned the book?
