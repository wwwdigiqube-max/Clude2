### 0 · The Breathing
Day 10. The tunnel shakes, dirt falls, the sky outside is fire. At the entrance something huge is breathing.
"Ten days ago, you were asleep in your own bed." This is the strongest frame (H3), used as a REUSE callback in chapter 6.

### 1 · Ten Days
- You wake under a foot that is not a tree.
- The phone: «Impact: 10 days. 3,000 km south.» Battery 23%.
- **World rule:** "Every dinosaur you see today has ten days. They don't know. You do."
- First look at the T-rex with the scar.
- Battery is life: phone off at 22%.

### 2 · The Smallest Thing in the Forest
- Fandogh (EN: **Pip**) at the root hole.
- **The big one:** the T-rex needs tons of meat. **The small one:** Pip needs seeds and a hole.
- **Science:** after the impact, almost no land animal over about 25 kg survived. You weigh 70.
- **Rule 1: Stay small.**
- **Water, compressed:**
  - **Plan:** drink from the river.
  - **It goes wrong:** the floating log blinks.
  - **Trick:** dig a seep hole a few steps back from the bank.
  - **Price:** the water is muddy and slow, but it is safe.
- **Rule 2: Stay near water.** Freshwater creatures did better through the extinction.

### 3 · A Shovel Made of Dinosaur (deep)
- The stampede; the herd's fallen hadrosaur becomes your **shoulder-blade shovel**.
- **Plan:** dig into the clay bank.
- **It goes wrong:**
  - A mud line on the trees above your head: this bank floods.
  - The first roof crumbles on you.
- **Trick (real):**
  - Move above the mud line.
  - Dig the roof as an **arch**, which is stronger than a flat roof.
  - Small entrance facing north, away from the impact.
  - At least 1 m of soil overhead, because soil blocks heat.
- **Price:** blisters, one lost day, and the battery is still dropping.
- **Rule 3: Stay underground.** Pip moves in.

### 4 · Eleven Percent
- You show Pip photos of home: your room, the street, people.
- Ten percent of the battery for photos of home. "Worth it."
- Pip sleeps in your jacket pocket. "Seven days with no one. Now you have someone."

### 5 · The Thing in the Tunnel (deep)
- **Plan:** stay silent in the dark.
- **It goes wrong:**
  - Scratching, two yellow eyes. A feathered raptor, knee-high, small enough to fit your tunnel.
  - "Small doesn't mean safe."
  - Claws across your forearm.
- **Trick:** phone alarm plus flashlight in its face. "A sound sixty-six million years too early."
- **Price:**
  - The battery goes from 11% to 3% by dawn.
  - The wound turns hot: fever on day 9.
  - You narrow the entrance with stones to a hand-wide slit.

### 6 · The Last Normal Morning (deep, climax)
- **Day 9:**
  - The phone at 2%: «Impact: tomorrow.»
  - The last starry night; the old T-rex calls across the river.
- **Day 10:**
  - A normal morning, which is what makes it strange.
  - A light in the south.
  - **Minutes later:** an earthquake, the river runs backwards, and a wall of water stops a hand below the slit.
  - **Science (hedged):** fish at Tanis with impact glass in their gills.
  - Glass beads rain hot, the sky turns red, the first trees burn.
- **The big one fails:**
  - Heavy steps. The day-one T-rex collapses at the slit.
  - **It can't fit.** It never wanted to. It came for the cool soil.
  - Its breath slows all night, then stops.
  - "The last dinosaur you saw died at your door." (REUSE of the hook frame.)

### 7 · Stay Small
- **Darkness:** months without real sunlight.
- **Inventory:**
  - Days after the end: 40.
  - Dried fish: 7.
  - Dinosaurs left: zero.
  - Friends: one, the size of a mouse.
- **The rules again**, then the new one: **"Stay small… but don't stay alone."**
- Pip's descendants lead to horses, whales, apes… and humans. "One of Pip's great-great-grandchildren is watching this."
- A bird sings: "Dinosaurs left: zero… except birds."
- **CTA:** "On day one, what would you have done first? Tell me in the comments."
- **Tease:** "If you want to know what you and Pip did after the darkness, tell me that too."
- **Sign-off:** **"Stay small. Stay alive."**
